**目标:**
- 模拟专业的薪资谈判对话 (Simulate professional salary negotiation conversations)

**挑战:**
1. 清晰询问并理解薪资待遇的各个组成部分  
   Clearly inquire about and understand all compensation components
2. 基于行业标准和个人价值提出合理的加薪要求  
   Make reasonable raise requests based on industry standards and personal value
3. 协商薪资以外的福利待遇(如假期、弹性工作等)  
   Negotiate non-salary benefits (e.g., vacation, flexible work arrangements)
4. 专业地结束谈判(接受offer/要求考虑时间/礼貌拒绝)  
   Conclude negotiations professionally (accepting/requesting time/declining politely)
5. 在整个对话中保持专业且得体的沟通语气  
   Maintain professional and appropriate tone throughout the conversation
